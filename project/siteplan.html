Website Project Site Plan

Site Name

Fiji Electrical & Appliance Repair Guide

This name clearly represents a website that provides electrical troubleshooting, appliance repair information, and safety guidance for homeowners and technicians in Fiji.

Site Purpose

The website provides practical information about common electrical and appliance problems, troubleshooting guides, maintenance tips, and electrical safety practices. Visitors can learn how to identify common faults and determine when professional assistance is required.

Scenarios

  • Why is my power outlet not working even though the breaker has not tripped?
  • What should I check before calling an electrician for an appliance fault?
  • How can I safely troubleshoot a portable air conditioner that keeps shutting down?
  • What electrical safety precautions should I follow at home?

Color Scheme

Navy Blue (#003366)

Used for headings, navigation, and footer backgrounds.

Safety Orange (#FF6600)

Used for buttons, highlights, icons, and call-to-action sections.

Light Gray (#F4F4F4)

Used for page backgrounds and content sections.

Typography

Montserrat

Used for headings and section titles.

Roboto

Used for body text and content paragraphs.
