Electrical Safety Tips

Following good electrical safety practices helps prevent fires, injuries, and damage to appliances.

Switch Off Power

Disconnect power before inspecting electrical equipment.

Use Proper Tools

Use insulated tools when working near electrical circuits.

Call Professionals

Serious faults should always be handled by licensed electricians.
