Electrical Safety Tips

Disconnect Power

Always disconnect power before inspecting equipment.

Use Proper Tools

Only use insulated tools when working near electricity.

Keep Areas Dry

Never work on electrical systems with wet hands.

Call Professionals

Complex repairs should be handled by qualified electricians.
